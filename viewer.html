A Book By Its Color

A series visualising every color described in world-famous books and screenplays.... The posters here represent where colour words are found across the text. Each row is a chapter, sized by the length of text relative to the whole book/script. You'll find iconic color references like the 'Heart of Gold' in the Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, the 'White Rabbit' and 'Red Roses' in Alice in Wonderland and sand described all across Dune.
